How the 30 days go

One week, one job

  1. Week 0. Find out what you're allowed to use.Check your work computer for Microsoft Copilot, check the approved software list, find out who approves new tools, and pick a task that fits what you found.
  2. Week 1. Build it.Fill out Describe Your Task, save those instructions in your tool, test them on a made-up example, then do one run with work information. There's a setup sheet for Copilot Chat, a Copilot agent, a Claude Project, and a Custom GPT.
  3. Week 2. Run it at work.Every time the task comes up, run it, log it in the tracker, and post one screenshot or before-and-after for review. When something goes wrong, there's a doc with the fix.
  4. Week 3. Fix it and build task two.Write whatever broke into your instructions, then pick a second task and build it the same way. You build, we review.
  5. Week 4. Turn it into proof.Total the tracker, write the resume line, and send your manager the email with the hours in it.

Every week has a Monday kickoff post, a Tuesday call with a recap, a Thursday check-in thread, and a Friday tracker post where everyone comments their hours.

Questions

Before you join the waitlist

What if my company blocks AI tools?

Week 0 is where you find out exactly what you're allowed to use. Nearly every company on Microsoft 365 has Microsoft Copilot Chat, which takes pasted text and returns text, and every build path in the cohort works from that. If you check and your company allows no AI tool at all, don't buy, because the promise depends on having one.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You write your task out in plain sentences on a template called Describe Your Task, and that document becomes the instructions the AI follows. There is no code anywhere in the cohort.

What if I don't have a job right now, or I freelance?

The cohort is built around a recurring task at a job, with a manager to send the results to. Without one there is nothing to automate and nothing to track, so it isn't for you yet. The free community is.

How much time does it take?

About 5 hours a week for 30 days. One call a week, one build, and the runs of your task, which you were already doing by hand.

What does the refund cover?
